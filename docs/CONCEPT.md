# Concept: "One Team. A Thousand Lights."

- **Metaphor:** Diwali falls on the new-moon night, the darkest of the year. The site is a palace courtyard that starts dark and is lit by the visitor. Every click adds light. By the finale the whole palace is lit, and everything the visitor lit earlier lights up together.
- **Type (Revision 1):** Cormorant Garamond Light is the carved inscription: large, tight, never bold, with italic for the one word that matters in each title. Jost is the engraved small print: tracked uppercase labels and light body copy. The headlines are large and quiet, and the jokes are small.
- **Colour:** Navy-to-plum night is about 85% of every screen. Gold hairlines are the only decoration, and ivory carries the text. Saffron is for flames and the single primary action. Magenta is a small accent in play only.
- **Signature motion:** The palace doors part slowly, the threshold diya ignites and one golden bloom carries the visitor through the arch into the sky. After that nothing moves unless the visitor moves it, until the finale.
- **Pacing:** The entrance is quiet and builds tension. Each destination has one focal point and lots of dark space. The finale is the one maximal moment: the palace lights, the sky fills, and then a single crisp line of thanks.
